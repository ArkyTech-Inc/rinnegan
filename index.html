Engineered for Autonomous Mobility

Addressing real-world physical barriers through edge-computed sensor fusion and voice spatial intelligence.

360° Obstacle Avoidance

Front-facing miniature ultrasonic array combined with micro LiDAR detects low-hanging objects, curbs, stairs, and moving vehicles up to 8 meters away.

Edge AI Object Recognition

Onboard Neural Processing Unit (NPU) translates visual scenes into instant natural voice descriptions: reading street signs, doorways, and identifying people.

Private Bone-Conduction

Direct temple vibration delivers directional audio navigation alerts while leaving the user's ears completely open to ambient sound cues for full safety.
